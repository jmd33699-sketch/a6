1. Purpose of Browser Cookies on CandleAppetite

Cookies are small text records deposited on your device to maintain session state, remember your selected interactive sommelier courses, and preserve search modal queries between page views.

2. Exhaustive Table of Deployed Cookies

Cookie Name Provider Classification Duration Operational Function
_ga Google Analytics Aggregated Analytics 24 Months Distinguishes anonymous visitor sessions to evaluate site navigation flow.
_ga_* Google Analytics Aggregated Analytics 24 Months Maintains session continuity across multi-page culinary visits.
ca_sommelier_pref CandleAppetite Functional Essential Session Stores selected tasting course and wine profile in the interactive sommelier.

3. Customizing Your Browser Cookie Settings

You can configure your browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge) to block all cookies or notify you whenever a cookie is written. Blocking essential cookies may reset your interactive sommelier calculations upon page refresh. For assistance, reach our technical team at contact@candleappetite.com.
